Dragon Ball Characters

Goku

Saiyan
Goku, the main protagonist of Dragon Ball, is a Saiyan warrior known for his strength and pure heart.

Vegeta

Saiyan Prince
Vegeta, the Prince of all Saiyans, is Goku's rival and ally, striving to surpass him in power.

Bulma

Scientist
Bulma, a brilliant scientist, is a key character who aids the Z Fighters with her inventions.

Frieza

Galactic Emperor
Frieza, the galactic emperor, is one of Goku's most formidable and iconic foes.
